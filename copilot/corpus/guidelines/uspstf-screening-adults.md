# Adult Preventive Screening (Practice Protocol)

Our clinic's screening checklist for average-risk adults, distilled from
current USPSTF grade A and B recommendations. Paraphrased for internal
use; individual recommendation statements at the source URL govern.

## Cancer screening

- **Colorectal cancer: all adults 45–75.** (Grade A for ages 50–75, grade B
  for 45–49; 2021 statement.) Acceptable strategies include colonoscopy
  every 10 years, FIT annually, or FIT-DNA every 1–3 years. Ages 76–85:
  selective, individualized.
- **Breast cancer: biennial screening mammography for women 40–74.**
  (Grade B, 2024 statement — starting age lowered from 50 to 40.)
  Evidence insufficient for 75 and older.
- **Cervical cancer: women 21–65.** (Grade A.) Ages 21–29 cytology every
  3 years; ages 30–65 cytology every 3 years, or hrHPV testing (alone or
  with cytology) every 5 years.
- **Lung cancer: annual low-dose CT for adults 50–80** with a 20 pack-year
  or greater smoking history who currently smoke or quit within the past
  15 years. (Grade B, 2021.)

## Cardiometabolic screening

- **Hypertension: all adults 18 and older** (grade A), with out-of-office
  confirmation before diagnosis.
- **Prediabetes and type 2 diabetes: adults 35–70 with overweight or
  obesity** (BMI 25 or higher; grade B, 2021). Reasonable to repeat about
  every 3 years if normal. Abnormal screens get preventive-intervention
  referral.
- **Statin eligibility assessment:** see `ascvd-statin-primary` for the
  risk-based protocol.
- **Abdominal aortic aneurysm: one-time ultrasound for men 65–75 who have
  ever smoked.** (Grade B.) For never-smoking men 65–75, selective (C);
  not recommended for women who never smoked.

## Infectious disease and behavioral health

- **HIV: everyone 15–65 at least once** (grade A); **hepatitis C: adults
  18–79 at least once** (grade B).
- **Depression: all adults**, including older adults and the postpartum
  period (grade B), with systems in place for follow-up.
- **Unhealthy alcohol use: adults 18 and older**, screening plus brief
  counseling (grade B). Tobacco: ask all adults, advise cessation, offer
  therapy (grade A).

## Bone health

- **Osteoporosis: women 65 and older** (grade B), and younger
  postmenopausal women at increased risk per a clinical risk assessment.

## How the Co-Pilot applies this

For each patient, screening due-ness is computed from **age, sex, and the
last documented completion date** — all three must come from the chart:

1. If a screening's completion is documented and in-window, it is not a
   gap; cite the record and the interval from this protocol.
2. If no completion is documented, report "no record in chart" — the test
   may exist outside our system. Phrase as "no mammogram on file," never
   "patient has not had a mammogram."
3. Risk-conditioned screenings (lung CT, AAA) require documented smoking
   history; when pack-years are absent, name the missing datum instead of
   assuming eligibility either way.
4. Never present a grade C or I service as due; this protocol includes
   only A/B services our practice has adopted.
