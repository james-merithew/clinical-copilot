# Adult Immunization Checklist (Practice Protocol)

Our clinic's adult vaccine checklist, distilled from the CDC/ACIP
recommended adult immunization schedule. Paraphrased for internal use;
the CDC schedule (including its footnotes) governs edge cases. The
schedule is updated at least annually — verify against the source before
protocol changes.

## Routine, age-based

- **Influenza: everyone, every season** (all persons 6 months and older
  without contraindication). For our panel this is a yearly checklist
  item every fall.
- **Tetanus/diphtheria: booster every 10 years.** At least one dose in
  adulthood should be Tdap (pertussis-containing); either Td or Tdap is
  acceptable for subsequent boosters.
- **Zoster (recombinant, Shingrix): 2 doses at age 50 and older**,
  2 to 6 months apart, regardless of prior varicella disease or live
  zoster vaccine. Also recommended for immunodeficient or
  immunosuppressed adults 19 and older.
- **Pneumococcal conjugate vaccine: all adults 50 and older** who have
  not received a recommended pneumococcal conjugate dose, and adults
  19–49 with risk conditions — which in our panel means diabetes,
  chronic kidney disease, chronic heart disease including CHF, and
  chronic lung or liver disease.
- **RSV: single dose for adults 75 and older**, and adults in their 50s
  through 74 who are at increased risk of severe RSV disease (chronic
  heart or lung disease, advanced CKD, diabetes with end-organ damage,
  frailty, long-term-care residence).
- **COVID-19:** follow the current-season CDC recommendation; our
  standing order mirrors the influenza workflow.

## Risk-condition triggers common in our panel

- **Diabetes (any age 19–59): hepatitis B series** if unvaccinated —
  universal adult HepB vaccination covers ages 19–59; at 60 and older
  with diabetes it is shared decision-making.
- **CKD / dialysis-track patients:** pneumococcal (above), hepatitis B
  (higher-dose formulations once approaching dialysis), and strict
  influenza coverage.
- **CHF and chronic cardiac disease:** pneumococcal and RSV eligibility
  as above.
- **Asplenia, immunosuppression, HIV, pregnancy** each carry their own
  additions — out of scope for this checklist; consult the CDC schedule
  footnotes directly.

## Documentation rules

Record vaccine, date, and source (administered here vs. patient-reported
vs. registry). Patient-reported doses without documentation count for
zoster and influenza pragmatics but not for hepatitis B series decisions,
where serology or re-vaccination applies.

## What the Co-Pilot should flag

1. **No influenza vaccine documented this season** (September onward) —
   one-line reminder.
2. **Age 50 or older with no pneumococcal conjugate dose on file**, or
   19–49 with diabetes/CKD/CHF and none on file.
3. **Age 50 or older with fewer than 2 recombinant zoster doses.**
4. **Diabetic patients 19–59 with no hepatitis B series documented.**
5. **Last tetanus-containing dose more than 10 years ago**, or no Tdap
   ever documented in adulthood.

Immunization history is frequently incomplete in the chart: always phrase
gaps as "no record on file," cite the immunization table rows that do
exist, and cite this protocol for the interval — never assert that a
vaccine was not given.
